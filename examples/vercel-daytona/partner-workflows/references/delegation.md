# Delegation with context

Start from the email the partner referenced. Read the full thread, relevant attachments, prior decisions, and matter history. Resolve the intended associate from the request or established matter team. If ambiguous, draft the work first and ask only for the recipient.

Produce a ready-to-use delegation:

**To:** [Associate]
**Matter:** [Matter]
**Do:** Concrete deliverable and due time, if supplied or evidenced.
**Context:** Relevant history and the last agreed position.
**Starting point:** Applicable internal precedent and the reason it applies.
**Constraints:** Client instructions, negotiated boundaries, budget or timing limits, and decisions to escalate.
**Sources:** Original thread and the specific documents/versions needed to act.

Attach context as concise summaries plus accessible source links. Do not claim files are attached unless the actual draft contains them. If the associate cannot access a linked document, flag that before sending; never broaden permissions automatically. Do not cross matter walls or attach another client's privileged documents merely because they are a useful precedent.

Distinguish confirmed constraints from suggestions. Missing precedent is "No verified precedent found," not an invented house position. If the partner only asked for a draft, return it in Telegram or create an email draft through the installed mail capability. Drafting does not authorize sending. Sending must use the existing messaging capability and its action controls after a specific instruction to send this delegation. Never mark an associate as assigned until delivery is confirmed.
