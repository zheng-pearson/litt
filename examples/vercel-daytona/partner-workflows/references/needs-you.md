# What needs you

Read all known active matters in the authorized scope, recent correspondence, deadlines, and outstanding decisions. Verify matter coverage before making an all-clear claim. Prioritize with assistant judgment: what requires this partner's authority, relationship, or judgment today?

Suppress routine progress, FYIs, already-delegated work moving normally, resolved items, dismissed unchanged items, and work the associate can finish without the partner. An unanswered email alone is not a partner decision. Keep an internal source-linked reason for suppressing a candidate so later "why didn't you flag this?" can be answered.

Lead with "[N] things need you today." Each numbered item contains matter, the specific decision or action, why today, and a short source link. Usually show up to four. Never hide a fifth genuinely urgent decision to satisfy the format: include it concisely. No progress recap, general advice, or invented deadlines.

Scheduled runs stay silent when nothing actionable changed. An unresolved decision may reappear when its deadline becomes material, with the reason stated. On an explicit "what needs me?" request, a verified empty result says "Nothing needs your decision today." If coverage is partial, say what was checked and which source is unavailable; do not say everything else is fine.

Record delivered decisions and evidence versions through shared matter memory. Resolving or delegating an item removes it from later feeds only when supported by the partner's instruction or source evidence, not because the assistant previously mentioned it.
