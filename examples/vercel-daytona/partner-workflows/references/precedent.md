# Internal precedent recall

Search authorized firm history, closed matter indexes, agreed documents, and negotiation records. This is internal practice recall, not public case-law research. Start with the clause and transaction context, then broaden terminology if needed. Do not silently substitute internet sources when firm history is unavailable.

For each plausible precedent, open the source and establish:

- Deal and date, transaction type/stage, side represented, and relevant governing context.
- Final executed or explicitly agreed clause, including cap basis, exclusions, baskets, survival, and carve-outs relevant to the question.
- Partner or associate who led it, if evidenced.
- Whether it reflects repeated practice, an exceptional concession, or only an unaccepted proposal.

Answer with the supported historical position, up to three closely matched deals, who ran them, exact source locators, and material differences. Use "In the two deals I could verify..." when the sample is small. One deal does not establish "our usual position." Conflicting precedents remain visible. Quote only the necessary clause fragment.

If no verified internal match exists, state that plainly and give the nearest authorized matches with limitations. Do not invent a cap, negotiation outcome, deal owner, or firm policy. Store concise source-linked precedent records through shared matter memory so later briefs and delegations can reuse them.
